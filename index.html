About Me

Feryal Haghjoo — Rasht

I fell in love with editing the same way most people fall in love with photography — by seeing a raw, ordinary frame quietly become something alive.

My work lives in the small decisions: a warmer highlight, a softened shadow, a color grade that finally matches the feeling of the moment. I specialize in color grading and portrait retouching, always keeping edits natural — enhancing what's already there instead of replacing it.

Every before-and-after in my portfolio tells a tiny story of patience. I work closely with photographers and clients across northern Iran and beyond, turning good photos into the ones people actually remember.
