About Me

Feryal Haghjoo — Rasht

I grew up between the rice fields and the fog of Gilan, where the light never sits still for long — and that is exactly what I chase with a camera.

My work moves between quiet documentary portraits and wide, atmospheric landscapes. I'm drawn to the in-between moments: the mist before it lifts, the coastline before the tourists arrive, the pause between two frames.

Over the past several years I've photographed across northern Iran and beyond, building a body of work rooted in place, patience, and natural light. Every series starts the same way — with walking, watching, and waiting for something true.
